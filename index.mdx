title: "Overview"
description: "Open-source multimodal redaction platform"
---

Nvisy is an open-source multimodal redaction platform. It detects and removes sensitive information across documents, images, and audio, combining deterministic patterns, NER, computer vision, and LLM-driven classification into auditable, policy-driven pipelines built for organizations that need every redaction to be reviewable and accountable.
